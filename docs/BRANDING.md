# Red Queen Branding Principles

## Identity

**Red Queen** is the public product/framework name. `WNHF` remains the historical development lineage and `wnhf` remains the technical Home Assistant domain for compatibility.

## Visual direction

Branding should be original, technical and independent. It may use abstract concepts such as orchestration, state, intelligence, topology, nodes or a queen/chess motif, but should remain its own visual identity.

Do not use third-party franchise logos, artwork, character likenesses, typography or other recognizable borrowed visual assets.

## Home Assistant assets

Brand assets are intentionally not part of the RC1 repository-preparation snapshot. They should be added only after the visual identity is finalized and validated against current Home Assistant/HACS requirements.
